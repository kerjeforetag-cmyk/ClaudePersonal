Your −10 °C question, answered

The NC-LI4880 sustains 1C continuous discharge at −10 °C with integrated cell heating drawing from the pack itself below 0 °C. Cold-chamber test protocol (TÜV SÜD, March 2026) is in your hub under Technical data.

Drop-in mechanical fit

DIN 43536 tray dimensions, ballasted to match lead-acid counterweight spec. Your Linde and Jungheinrich trucks keep their type approval — confirmation letters included.

Charging-room compliance

Opportunity charging removes the classified battery room entirely. SmartCharge installs meet VDE 0510-48; ventilation calculations for Petra Vogel's review are pre-filled in the hub.

Warranty that matches the math

7 years / 10,000 cycles to 80% capacity — the same horizon as the TCO model, so the savings curve is underwritten, not hoped for.
