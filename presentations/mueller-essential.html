Summary

We propose converting 96 forklifts at your Ludwigsburg site from lead-acid to NordCell Li-ion with opportunity charging. The change removes all daily battery swaps, frees the 410 m² battery room, and reduces seven-year cost of ownership by an estimated €505,000.

Scope of delivery

  • 72 × NC-LI4880 Li-ion packs (48V / 800Ah) and 24 × NC-LI4860 (48V / 600Ah) — direct drop-in, no truck modification.
  • 14 × SmartCharge 30 kW opportunity chargers positioned at break areas and dock ends.
  • FleetView telemetry on all 96 packs, 3-year license: state of health, utilization and Phase-2 sizing data.
  • Installation across two weekends, commissioning, and one on-site training day. No production downtime.

Technical notes

  • Cold performance: NC-LI4880 sustains 1C continuous discharge at −10 °C (TÜV SÜD cold-chamber protocol, March 2026 — available in your hub).
  • Compliance: chargers meet VDE 0510-48; opportunity charging removes the classified battery-room requirement.
  • Warranty: 7 years / 10,000 cycles to 80% capacity.
