FAQ

Fair questions.

Do my customers need a Mimra account?

No. You send one link; it opens their hub in a customer view — prices, documents, proposals and decks, nothing internal. They can even build a quote and request it as a proposal.

What format are the presentations?

Standalone HTML files. They open in any browser, animate smoothly, work offline once loaded, and print cleanly to PDF. No PowerPoint, no plugins, nothing to install.

Which industries does it fit?

Any business-to-business sale with real products, real prices and real relationships — industrial equipment, components, software, services. The demo tenant sells battery systems; swap the data and it's yours.

What's the difference between Essential and Signature decks?

Essential is a clean, fast template filled from your hub data — included in the plan. Signature composes the deck for that specific customer: narrative, charts and layout built around their numbers and even the questions they asked in the hub. It's the one you close with.

Is this a live product?

This site is a working product demo with fictional data. The full app — hubs, studio, forecast, everything — runs right in your browser. Try it.
